Macher chop (Fish Croquettes)
This is one of my favourite snacks in the evening along with tea. You can get this chop from many fast food centers in West Bengal. But today you can make this Bengali style macher chop in your own kitchen. Just follow the recipe and you will get this mouth watering snack very easily.
 
Dry Chili Fish with Mushroom
This is a very simple and easy recipe. You can serve this as starter or main course. You can also substitute fish with chicken. It tastes delicious. Try this at your home and tell me about your experience.
 
Kheema Biriyani (Minced meat Biriyani)
In India "biriyani" is a very popular dish. There are plenty of variations in biriyani. The process is a little lenthy but it tastes delicious. Yesterday I took an attempt on making Kheema biriyani, after the entire process I realize it is the easiest one. That is why I am sharing this recipe with you because it is one of the tastiest and easiest biriyani ever. Try this at your home and make yours ...
 
Chicken Pakora
How about a crazy snack in a rainy evening ?? Ohh yeaah its Pakora time !! Many houses have their own pakora story. In Kolkata we get this kind of chicken pakora from fast food centers. If you follow this recipe you can get the same flavour from your homemade pakora.
 
Mutton Pulao / Yakhni Pulao
This recipe is an authentic pulao recipe. In any gathering you can make this dish and it will be blast. This pulao is super tasty and very easy to make. You just have to be perfect about the measurement. Please try this at your home.
 
Stawberry Milkshake
How about a chilled milkshake in a crazy hot afternoon?? Everybody will say "yessss" .. Lets do this with the sweet flavour of strawberry.
 
Dahi Chicken (Ma's recipe)
"Lajawab" is the only compliment about mom's recipe. My mom make this dish in a very easy and simple way. But it tastes delicious. You will need very few ingredients for this dish. This the best recipe for teenagers because its very easy to make.
 
Lassi (Yogurt smoothie)
Lassi is the most popular drink of Punjab, India. Nowadays we can get lassi almost everywhere. This is a very refreshing drink during summer. Flavoured lassi are also easily available in the market. But I personally prefer plain lassi. Just follow this recipe and you will get the best lassi from your kitchen.
 
Singara (samosa)
In India samosa is a very popular appetizer. We call it "singara" in West Bengal. Almost every evening my grandpa brought samosas from roadside shop and they were delicious. Last week I made samosas at home (inspired by my mother & husband) and they came out really tasty. So, please try this recipe at your home and impress your family.
 
Hara Bhara Kabab (Green Vegetable Kabab)
Yesterday my husband was craving for some snacks in afternoon. I had some boiled potatoes in my fridge. Then I thought about this recipe. You can say it is fried kabab. "Hara" means green color. After eating this he said you should upload the recipe ☺️. So, its very easy to make. Just give it a try.
