Chicken Malai kebab

What can I say about this one? Because again its Kabab!! This malai kabab has very smooth texture and smokey flavour. You will be lost whenever you put that juicy, tendor piece of chicken in your mouth. It will satisfy your appetite .




Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken 20 cubes.
  • Cream cheese half bowl.
  • Heavy cream 3 Teaspoon.
  • Black pepper powder 2 Teaspoon.
  • Ginger and garlic paste 2 Teaspoon.
  • Hung curd 4 Teaspoon.
  • Lemon juice 2 Teaspoon.
  • Salt as per your taste.
  • Oil 2 Teaspoon.
  • Butter 2 cubes.
  • Bamboo skewers 4 pieces.
  • Cumin powder 1 Teaspoon.
  • Coriander powder 1 Teaspoon.

Steps

  • Marinate the chicken cubes with curd, ginger and garlic paste, cream cheese, heavy cream, black pepper powder, salt, lemon juice, cumin and coriander powder. Keep it in the refrigerator for 3-4 hours.
  • Soak the bamboo skewers in water for 30 minutes.
  • Preheat the oven 500degree f for 10 minutes.
  • Pierce the chicken pieces one by one with the skewer. Put 5 chicken pieces in 1 skewer.
  • Place the skewers in the oven upper rack. The skewers should be close to the filaments. Keep it for 7 minutes in 450 d f.
  • Then turn the skewers other side and brush some melted butter. Again put this in the oven for 7 minutes.
  • After that remove the skewers from the oven rack and sprinkle some lime juice.

Your Chicken malai kebab is ready ...!!!
  • Serve hot with Green Chatney.
