Hot & Sour Soup (Chicken & Egg)

 (Non-Vegetarian)

I love to eat soup. When its 'hot and sour' my hand will always up for this. You can categorize this soup as chinese starter. One can eat lots of vegetables, chicken and egg through a bowl of this hot soup. If you like spicy you have to try this one.




Ingredients


  • Minced chicken one cup.
  • One egg.
  • Finely chopped (onion, different colors of capsicum, green beans, carrots, green chilies, spring onion and one stick of celery).
  • One clove of garlic chopped.
  • Light soy sauce 4 tsp.
  • Chili garlic sauce 3 tsp.
  • Corn flour 2 tsp.
  • Salt and sugar.
  • Ground black pepper 3 tsp.
  • Msg 1 tsp.
  • White oil 3 tsp.
  • Vinegar 2 tsp.
  • Butter 2 small cubes.
  • Lots of water.

Steps


  • Soak some chopped green chilies in vinegar for a hour.
  • Take a pressure cooker. Heat the oil.
  • Add the chopped garlic. Saute it for a minute.
  • Now add all the vegetables. Mix it very well in high flame for 5 minutes.
  • Now add the chicken. Mix it well.
  • Now add soy sauce, chili garlic sauce, black pepper, msg, chili vinegar some salt and a pinch of sugar. Mix it very well for 4 minutes.
  • Add 5 cups of water. Mix it well.
  • Put the lid on and pressure this soup till 3 slow whistles.
  • Remove the lid and check the soup. Everything should be fully cooked.
  • Now again let this soup boiling.
  • Dilute cornflour in 5 tsp water. Add this in the boiling soup and stir this very well.
  • After 3 minutes take a bowl. Break a egg. Beat the egg with a fork.
  • Now add the beaten egg into the soup when its boiling. Stir this continuously.
  • Add the butter.
  • Check the taste. It should be spicy and little bit of sour. Turn off your gas.

Your hot and sour soup is ready ...
  • Sprinkle some spring onion and chili garlic sauce on top of the soup and enjoy ...!!!
