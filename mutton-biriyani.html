Mutton Biriyani

 (Non-Vegetarian)

Its a famous non vegetarian main course dish. In India biriyani is a very popular item. Hope you like it.




Ingredients


  • Mutton medium size 6 pieces.
  • Basmati rice 4 cups.
  • Two small potatoes.
  • One onion sliced.
  • Ginger and garlic paste 2 tsp.
  • Curd 4 tsp.
  • Biriyani masala 4 tsp.( from any indian store).
  • Cumin seed 1 tsp.
  • Green cardemom 5.
  • Six cloves.
  • One star anis.
  • One small cinnamon stick.
  • Yellow food colour.
  • Two green chilies.
  • Clarified butter/ghee 8 tsp.
  • Salt and sugar.
  • Water.

Steps


  • Marinate the mutton pieces with curd, ginger and garlic paste for 3 hours.
  • Fry the potatoes and keep aside.
  • Take a pan. Heat 4 tsp ghee.
  • Add sliced onion. Fry it till golden brown.
  • Add marinated mutton. Mix it well.
  • Add salt, biriyani masala, green chilies. Mix it very well for 5 minutes.
  • Now add some luke warm water and cook this until Mutton pieces are fully done.
  • Dont make it too much runny or dry. Make some thick gravy .
  • Turn of the gas.
  • Now wash the basmati rice in cold water very well.
  • Boil some water with cumin seeds, green cardemom, cloves, cinnamon stick, star anis and 4 tsp salt.
  • Add the rice in boiling water. Chech the rice every two minutes.
  • When the rice 80% done drain the water.
  • Now take vessel. Dont turn on the gas. Put 1 tsp ghee.
  • Then put a layer of mutton and fried potatoes, then a layer of rice. Repeat the process twice. Add 3 tsp ghee and pinch of food colour on top of rice.
  • Cover the vessel with a very tight lid. Turn on the gas and keep the vessel on gas for 20 minutes in medium flame.
  • Then turn off the gas and keep it uncover for another 10 minutes. After that open the vessel.

Your mutton biriyani is ready .... Enjoy ...!!
  • Serve hot with raita, gravy or any type of curry.
