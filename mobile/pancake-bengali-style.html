Pancake (Bengali Style)

Kids are always excited about their lunchbox. My mom used to make this dish for my lunchbox and I just loved it. Yesterday I made this in dinner and we two as usual loved it. I mentioned the style here because it is little bit of spicy but very easy to make. Try this at your home and share with your friends.




Ingredients

  • 2 cups of all purpose flour.
  • 1 egg.
  • 3 cups of milk.
  • Half tsp baking powder.
  • Half onion finely chopped.
  • 2 green chilies chopped.
  • 1 tsp chopped garlic.
  • Salt.
  • White oil.

Steps

  • Take a bowl. Add egg, salt and 1 cup of milk. Beat it very well.
  • Then gradually add little flour and mix it. Do not add all flour and milk at a time. Add little portion of flour and milk , then mix.
  • After mixing 2 cups of flour with milk add onion, garlic, green chilies, baking powder and salt. Mix this as a lump free runny batter.
  • Heat 2 tsp oil in a frying pan. Add little portion of batter and spread with spoon. Fry one side for 4 minutes.
  • Then turn it and fry this side for another 3-4 minutes. You can make 4-5 pancakes with this batter.

Your Bengali style Pancake is ready ..
  • Enjoy this hot with some ketchup / chutney ...
