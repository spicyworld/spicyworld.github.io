Butter Chicken

I have someone in my house who frequently demands to eat 'homemade-restaurant style' dinner in weekend 😉. That's why last Sunday I made 'butter chicken' along with 'butter naan'. Nowadays 'butter chicken' is famous in all over the world. In Indian restaurants this is the mostly ordered dish. It has a tomato based, nice, rich, velvety gravy and tandoori flavored chicken pieces. The combination was a total hit but you can also serve this with 'jeera rice', 'pulaao' or 'fried rice'. In this dish you will need tandoori chicken, I have already posted the recipe, click here to find that. Try this in your kitchen and serve a 'restaurant style dinner' to your family.




Ingredients

  • 2 big legs of tandoori chicken.
  • 3 big tomatoes.
  • 1 tsp ginger and garlic paste.
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds.
  • Spice powder (2 tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp turmeric powder, 1 tsp cumin powder, 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp garam masala powder).
  • 2 tablespoons of dry fenugreek leaves.
  • 4 tablespoons of butter.
  • 6 tsp white oil.
  • Half cup of heavy cream.
  • Salt and sugar.
  • Warm water.

Steps

  • Put the tomatoes in some boiling water for 2 minutes. Drain the water and peel the skin of the tomatoes. Then blend it in a smooth paste.
  • Heat oil and butter in a pan.
  • Add cumin seeds and 1 tablespoon of crushed dry fenugreek leaves in hot oil. Saute for a minute.
  • Then add ginger and garlic paste and all of the above mentioned spice powder. Cook this for 2 minutes in medium flame.
  • Add tomato paste in the pan along with some salt and 1 tsp of sugar. Mix this for 5 minutes.
  • Then add some warm water and cook it for 15 minutes.
  • After that add the tandoori chicken pieces into the gravy. Mix it very well and cook it for another 10 minutes.
  • Check the seasoning and add the remaining fenugreek leaves, heavy cream. Mix it for 3 minutes.
  • Turn off your gas and let it rest for 5 minutes.

Your Butter chicken is ready ...
  • Enjoy this hot with butter naan/ jeera rice/ pulaao ...
