Mutton Kosha (bengali style)

(Non-Vegetarian)

Its a delicious nonveg main course dish. This is a Bengali style recipe. In many gatherings they make this dish. Its a very popular item. You should try this.




Ingredients

  • Mutton 15 pieces.
  • Potato 4 pieces.
  • One big onion chopped.
  • Ginger&garlic paste 2 tsp.
  • One small tomato chopped.
  • Hunk curd 5 tsp.
  • Turmeric powder 2 tsp.
  • Red chili powder 1 tsp.
  • Coriander powder 2 tsp.
  • Cumin powder 2 tsp.
  • Mace 1 blade.
  • Nutmeg half tsp.
  • Five green cardemom.
  • Four cloves.
  • One small cinnamon stick.
  • Six black pepper corn.
  • One bay leaf.
  • Two green chilies.
  • Mustard oil 6 tsp.
  • Salt & sugar.
  • Warm water.

Steps

  • Marinate the mutton pieces with curd and 1 tsp of ginger&garlic paste. Keep aside for 2 hours.
  • Lightly fry the potatoes. Keep aside.
  • Take a pressure cooker and heat mustard oil.
  • Add 2 tsp sugar in the hot oil and caramalized it.
  • Then add bay leaf, green cardemom, black pepper, cloves, cinnamon stick. Saute it for a minute.
  • Add choppes onion. Fry this till golden brown.
  • Add 1 tsp ginger & garlic paste. Cook this for 2 minutes.
  • Then add chopped tomatoes. Cook this for 5 minutes.
  • Now add salt, all the powders, mace & nutmeg. Mix it for 1 minute.
  • Then add the marinated mutton. Mix it very well. Then cook it in slow flame with lid for 20 minutes. Mutton will leave its own juice.
  • After that add some warm water, green chilies. Pressure this till 4 slow whistles.
  • Then add the fried potatoes and again pressure this till 3 slow whistles.
  • Mutton should have some thick gravy.

Your mutton kosha is ready.
  • Serve this with hot plain rice, pulao, naan ...
