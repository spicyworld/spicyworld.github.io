Palak Paneer

'Palak paneer' is a famous north indian dish. This is a hit item for spinach lovers. After adding heavy cream the texture will make you greedy. Palak paneer's smooth, rich and velvety gravy is beyond everything. It goes very well with butter naan.




Ingredients

  • ten paneer cubes
  • one bunch spinach leaves
  • two tomatoes
  • one inch ginger
  • two green chilis
  • cumin seeds 1 Teaspoon
  • turmeric powder ½ Teaspoon
  • coriander powder 1 Teaspoon
  • cumin powder 1 Teaspoon
  • red chili powder ½ Teaspoon
  • bengal gram flour 2 Teaspoon
  • garam masala ½ Teaspoon
  • dry fenugreek leaves ½ Teaspoon
  • heavy cream 3 Teaspoon
  • salt and sugar as per your taste
  • ½ a glass of warm water
  • oil 2 Teaspoon
  • butter 1 Teaspoon

Steps

  • Make a paste of no.3 to no.5. Keep aside.
  • Then make another paste of only spinach leaves and 6 Teaspoons of water.
  • Take a pan. Heat 2 Teaspoons oil and 1 Teaspoon butter.
  • After melting the butter add cumin seeds.
  • Then add no.7 to no.11. Mix it.
  • Then add the tomato paste , salt and sugar.Cook it for 15 minutes.
  • When oil starts separating add spinach paste. Cook it for another 15 minutes.
  • Then add some water for gravy. Cook it for 5 minutes.
  • After that add heavy cream. Mix it for 2 minutes.
  • Add garam masala and dry fenugreek leaves. Mix it one more time.

Your Palak paneer is ready.
  • Serve hot with roti, naan. paratha, kulcha or rice.
