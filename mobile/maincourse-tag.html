Butter Chicken (NonVeg Item)
I have someone in my house who frequently demands to eat 'homemade-restaurant style' dinner in weekend 😉. That's why last Sunday I made 'butter chicken' along with 'butter naan'. Nowadays 'butter chicken' is famous in all over the world. In Indian restaurants this is the mostly ordered dish. It has a tomato based, nice, rich, velvety gravy and tandoori flavored chicken pieces. The combination was a total hit but you can also serve this with 'jeera rice', 'pulaao' or 'fried rice'. In this dish you will need tandoori chicken, I have already posted the recipe, click here to find that. Try this in your kitchen and serve a 'restaurant style dinner' to your family.
Dum Aloo (Veg Item)
We all love to eat 'aloor dom' along with 'luchi' or 'kochuri'. This is our comfort food. It also goes very well with 'pulao' or 'fried rice'. This one is my mom's recipe. She make the tastiest aloor dom ever. Yesterday I tried this recipe and it came out perfectly.Just give it a try.
Mutton Korma (NonVeg Item)
Last weekend I had a party in my house. Among all the foods, the main attraction was Mutton Korma. This dish is very rich itself. Always serve this with light rice item. Just give it a try.
