Chicken Chaap (NonVeg Item)
After researching for a long time on the authentic recipe of Chicken chaap, here is the way how I made it. This recipe will give you the exact flavour of restaurant style Chaap. Do not include this in your regular diet chart. It has a really rich gravy and goes best with pulao, biriyani, naan or roti. Try not to avoid any ingredient from the list otherwise you will not achieve the real taste.
