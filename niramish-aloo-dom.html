Niramish aloor dom / Veg dum aloo

Usually when we offer a meal to god / goddess with our respect and devotion, we call it 'Bhog' / 'prosad' . In any 'bhoger prosad' this 'aloor dom' is a must with 'khichdi and chutney'. This recipe is without onion and garlic, that's why I mentioned 'niramish'. During monsoon you can also enjoy this with khichuri, beguni and chutney or in weekend breakfast make some 'luchi' / poori with this curry. We, Bengalis love these combos. Whenever you arrange your next 'puja' in your home, don't forget to put this item in your 'bhog' / 'prasad'. I have already posted some other 'dum aloo' recipe also. Try this in your kitchen and you will love it.




Ingredients

  • 3 to 4 potatoes cut into 4 pieces.
  • 2 big tomatoes.
  • 3 green chilies.
  • 1 Teaspoon ginger paste.
  • Whole spices ( 4 green cardamoms, 4 cloves, 1 dry red chilli, 1 bay leaf, 1 small cinnamon stick).
  • 1 Teaspoon turmeric powder.
  • 1 Teaspoon red chilli powder.
  • 1 Teaspoon garam masala powder.
  • 3 to 4 Tablespoons of mustard oil.
  • Salt and sugar.
  • Warm water.



Steps

Heat oil in a kadai.
Add the whole spices and 1 Teaspoon sugar. Saute them for a minute. The sugar will caramalize.
Then add ginger paste. Mix it for 30 seconds.
Then add chopped tomatoes and 1 Teaspoon salt. Cook it in medium flame for 4 minutes.
When the tomatoes become mushy add turmeric, red chilli and garam masala powder. Mix it till the oil separates.
Then add potato cubes and green chilies. Mix them with the masala very well for 5 minutes.
Add some warm water, some salt and cook till the potatoes become tendor.
Check the seasoning and adjust it according to your taste.
There should be some gravy.

Your niramish aloor dom is ready ...
  • Enjoy this hot with khichuri, luchi or paratha ...
