Ghugni or Yellow peas curry

 (Vegetarian)

It is almost impossible to find them, who does not love street food and specially 'ghugni' from train vendors. But 'ghugni' also plays a different role along with 'nimki'and 'naaru' during BIJOYA dashami. From childhood I am a big fan of my mom's cooking. I think you already understand that this one is also from my mom's diary :-)




Ingredients


  • Yellow peas 1 small bowl.
  • Onion 1 big size
  • Ginger garlic paste 1 tsp.
  • Tomato 1 medium size.
  • Two green chilies.
  • Cumin seeds 1 tsp.
  • Roasted cumin powder 1 tsp.
  • Roasted coriander powder 1 tsp.
  • Red chili powder 1 tsp.
  • Garam masala powder 1 tsp.
  • Salt and suger as per your taste.
  • Water.
  • Mustard oil 4 tsp.
  • Turmeric powder 2 tsp.
  • One bay leaf.
  • One dry red chili.

Steps


  • Soak the yellow peas overnight into the water.
  • Take a pressure cooker with water and boil the soaked peas till one whistle.
  • Now take a pan and heat mustard oil.
  • Add cumin seeds, bay leave and dry red chili. Saute it for a minute.
  • Add the chopped onion. Fry it for 7 mins.
  • Then add the ginger and garlic paste. Cook it for 4 minutes.
  • Add the chopped tomato. Cook it for 5 minutes.
  • Now add turmeric, redchili, cumin and coriander powder. Cook it for 5 minutes.
  • Add salt and sugar as per your taste.
  • When oil starts separating add boiled peas and green chilies. Cook this for 10 minuts.
  • If you want this runny then add some water.
  • When its done add some chopped coriander leaves, 1 tsp ghee, roasted cumin powder and some garam masala powder.

Your ghugni is ready to serve.
  • Serve hot with roti, naan.
