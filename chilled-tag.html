Lassi (Yogurt smoothie)
Lassi is the most popular drink of Punjab, India. Nowadays we can get lassi almost everywhere. Its a very refreshing drink during summer. Flavoured lassi are also easily available in the market. But I personally prefer plain lassi. Just follow this recipe and you will get the best lassi from your kitchen.
 
Watermelon Smoothie
I love to eat chilled watermelon cubes during summer. My divaai(grandma) serve watermelon cubes with some crushed ice on top and then magically the taste level increases twice. She has her own unique style. So, today I made an attempt on watermelon smoothie and it came out quite delicious. Hope you will like it.
