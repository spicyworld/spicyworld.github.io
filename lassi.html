Lassi (Yogurt smoothie)

Lassi is the most popular drink of Punjab, India. Nowadays we can get lassi almost everywhere. This is a very refreshing drink during summer. Flavoured lassi are also easily available in the market. But I personally prefer plain lassi. Just follow this recipe and you will get the best lassi from your kitchen.




Ingredients

  • 2 cups of plain yogurt.
  • 1 cup of chilled milk.
  • 3 Teaspoons of powdered sugar.
  • 1 Teaspoon of rose water.
  • A pinch of salt.

Steps

  • Take a blender. Put the yogurt, milk and sugar. Blend it for 2 minutes in high speed.
  • Then add rose water and salt. Again blend it for 2 minutes.

Your Lassi is ready ...
  • Enjoy this supercool drink in summer afternoon ...
