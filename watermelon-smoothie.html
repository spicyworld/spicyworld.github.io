Watermelon Smoothie

 (Vegetarian)

I love to eat chilled watermelon cubes during summer. My divaai(grandma) serve watermelon cubes with some crushed ice on top and then magically the taste level increases twice. She has her own unique style. So, today I made an attempt on watermelon smoothie and it came out quite delicious. Hope you will like it.




Ingredients

  • Two cups of freezed watermelon cubes.
  • Lemon juice 4 tsp.
  • Sugar 3 tsp.
  • Black salt half tsp.
  • Mint leaves 3-4.
  • Finely chopped half tsp ginger.

Steps

  • Cut the watermelon in cube sizes. Then put it in the freezer for 1 hour.
  • Take a blender. Put all the ingredients. Blend it very well.
  • Check the seasoning. Adjust the sweetness according to your taste.

Your watermelon smoothie is ready ...
  • Enjoy this as summer cooler ...
