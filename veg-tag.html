Vegetable Biriyani
This dish dont need any explanation. Because its 'Biriyani'. Its the most popular dish in India. The vegetarian version of biriyani is also very tasty Just give a try and impress your family.
 
Hot & Sour Soup (Chicken & Egg)
I love to eat soup. When its 'hot and sour' my hand will always up for this. You can categorize this soup as chinese starter. One can eat lots of vegetables, chicken and egg through a bowl of this hot soup. If you like spicy you have to try this one.
 
Fried Rice
There are many varities of fried rice you can make like 'chinese style', 'indian style', 'fried rice with sauce' etc. This recipe is about indian style. Its a complete meal with vegetables. You can add chicken, egg, prwan, mushroom in this dish. You will love this dish.
 
Chana masala
I have a friend in New Jersy, Monica, who makes the best chana masala ever. I learned this recipe from her. Since we moved from Jersy I missed her and her cooking very much. Its a punjabi dish, goes very well with bhatura, puri or roti. The recipe is very simple and it tastes delicious.
 
Paneer Makhani
'Makhni' is a name of rich, smooth, creamy gravy which is very popular in India. You can make 'chicken makhni', 'paneer makhni', 'kofta curry' and many more dishes with this gravy. I personally love the methi flavour and creaminess about this gravy. We had it in dinner last night with soft butter naans. The combination was heavenly. You just have to try this.
